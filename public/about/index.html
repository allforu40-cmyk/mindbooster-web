Our Story

Founded in Barcelona in 2022

From business know-how to applied AI

Mind Booster was founded in Barcelona in 2022 to help companies grow by putting artificial intelligence to work in a practical way. Our team brings together experience in hospitality, technology and business development.

We use that experience for our clients and for the ventures we build ourselves: Chingolandia, Mosty Restaurant & Lounge, Devoctura, Nexasmus, Changarro and Seekers.

Understand, Build, Grow

Who We Are

Mind Booster is an AI consultancy and venture builder based in Barcelona. We help companies reach their goals by understanding their business first and then applying the AI solutions that fit.

Our Journey

We started in 2022 with a simple idea: AI should be practical and affordable for every business. Since then we have advised companies and launched our own ventures in hospitality, food and technology to prove what we recommend.

Our Mission

To make AI work for real businesses, with clear advice, tailored software and honest feasibility studies, so every investment in technology delivers a measurable return.

Our Expertise

AI strategy, custom AI software, consulting and feasibility studies, and hands-on experience running hospitality and technology businesses.

Our Values

Practical over hype. Open about costs and risks. Focused on results we can measure together with our clients.

Our Approach

Understand, build, grow

We start by learning how your business works and where it wants to go. Then we design the right AI solution, build it without unnecessary cost, and stay with you as it grows. We follow the same approach in our own ventures, so every recommendation is one we would make for ourselves.
