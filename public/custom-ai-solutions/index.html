What we build

Every solution is designed for one client and one goal. These are the kinds of things we build most often.

AI assistants and chatbots

Assistants trained on your own documents, products and policies that answer customers on your website or WhatsApp, or help your team find answers instantly.

Workflow automation

Invoices, emails, orders, bookings and data entry handled automatically, so your people spend their time on work that needs them.

Document and data intelligence

Read, classify and extract information from PDFs, contracts, forms and spreadsheets, and search everything your company knows in seconds.

Forecasting and analytics

Predict demand, sales, stock and staffing needs from your own data, with dashboards that show what to do next.

AI for sales and marketing

Lead qualification, personalised follow-ups and on-brand content created faster, with a person always in control.

Integration with your tools

Connected to what you already use: CRM, ERP, POS, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Shopify and more. No need to change how you work.

How it works

A clear process with a fixed-price proposal before any build starts.

  1. Discovery call

    A free conversation about your business, your goals and the tasks that cost you the most time.

  2. Needs analysis

    We map your processes and data and pick the use cases with the biggest return.

  3. Proposal

    A tailored solution design with scope, timeline, fixed price and the results we will measure.

  4. Prototype

    A first working version tested with your real data, so you see the value before the full build.

  5. Build and integrate

    We develop the solution, connect it to your tools and test it with your team.

  6. Launch and improve

    Training for your team, then support and continuous improvement as your needs grow.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a technical team?

No. We handle the design, development and integration, and we train your team to use the solution day to day.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. A pilot is the fastest way to start; the proposal includes a clear timeline before any work begins.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so after the discovery call and needs analysis you receive a fixed-price proposal with no surprises.

Who owns the solution?

You do. The solution is built for your business, and the ownership and licensing terms are set out clearly in the proposal.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We design every solution with GDPR in mind and agree with you where data is stored and who can access it.
