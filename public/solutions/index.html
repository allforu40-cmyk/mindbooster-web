What We Do

AI Strategy & Business Growth

Founded in Barcelona in 2022, Mind Booster helps companies reach their objectives with artificial intelligence.

We start by understanding your business: your goals, your processes, your customers and your data. Then we identify where AI can make the biggest difference and apply the solution that fits, not a generic playbook.

Every recommendation is tied to a clear, measurable outcome, so you always know what AI is doing for your business.

Custom AI Software

We design and build AI software tailored to your company, based on what you actually need.

You bring the problem; we handle the research into which models, tools and architecture fit best. You skip months of trial and error, avoid paying for features you don’t use, and save significantly on development costs.

From internal assistants and workflow automation to customer-facing AI products, we deliver software that fits the way you work and scales as you grow.

Consulting & Feasibility Studies

Before you invest, know what will work.

Our consultants work with you to evaluate new ideas, AI projects and technology investments. Our feasibility studies cover technical viability, market fit, costs, risks and expected return.

You get a clear, honest picture and a practical roadmap, so you can make decisions with confidence.

Hospitality: AI, Restaurants & Franchising

We know hospitality from the inside. Through the Mind Booster Hospitality Group we run and franchise restaurants and cloud kitchens, including Mosty Restaurant & Lounge and Chingolandia, and we bring that hands-on experience to every client.

AI for restaurants, hotels and venues: demand forecasting and smarter pricing, bookings and guest messages answered around the clock, inventory planning that cuts food waste, and staff scheduling where it is needed most.

Restaurant and cloud-kitchen management: turnkey operations, standardised modular layouts that get a new location open in 3 to 6 months, seamless POS integration and partnerships with the main delivery platforms.

Franchising and expansion: a hybrid franchise model with single-unit, multi-unit, fractional and company-led openings, backed by a 4–6 week training academy, centralised marketing, bulk purchasing, 24/7 support and regular quality audits, all the way to master franchise agreements for international markets.
