Changarro

Changarro is the back office for independent restaurants, made in Barcelona. Snap a photo of the supplier’s delivery note and Changarro does the rest: stock updates itself, every dish is re-costed and you get an alert when a supplier raises a price.

  • Delivery notes and stock: scan with your phone, check what arrived and stock is updated, including expiry dates, transfers between kitchens and stock counts.
  • Recipe costing: the real cost and margin of every dish, using the last price you paid, with recipe cards ready to print.
  • Prices and suppliers: price-rise alerts showing what they cost you each month, plus quotes from alternative suppliers in your city.
  • Clock-in: a working-hours record on a shared tablet, with a PIN for each person, rotas and a monthly sheet to sign.

Plans from €35 per location per month + VAT, with a 15-day free trial. POS integration is coming soon, and all data is stored in the EU.

WebsiteInstagram @changarro.eshola@changarro.es
