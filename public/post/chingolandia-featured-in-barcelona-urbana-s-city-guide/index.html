Chingolandia featured in Barcelona Urbana’s city guide

Barcelona, 9 November 2026. Barcelona Urbana, the Barcelona city guide, has published a long-form piece on Chingolandia, the Mexican restaurant in Sant Antoni known for its beef birria. Written by editor Reinel González, the article runs in English, Spanish and Catalan.

The piece walks a first-time visitor through the whole experience: what birria is and how to eat it, what to order on a first visit, what dinner costs, opening hours, group and Christmas menus, vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options, and how to reach the restaurant from the metro.

“Start with the quesabirria: crisp, cheesy tortillas filled with birria and served with a cup of broth to dip each bite in.” — Barcelona Urbana, 9 November 2026

Chingolandia serves beef birria three ways — as tacos, as quesabirria with a cup of broth for dipping, and as consommé with tortillas — each at €16. The restaurant holds 4.6 stars on Google from more than 850 reviews.

The article was published in collaboration with Chingolandia and is labelled as such by Barcelona Urbana.

About Chingolandia

Chingolandia is a Mexican restaurant at Carrer de Floridablanca 108, in the Sant Antoni neighbourhood of Barcelona, a two-minute walk from Sant Antoni metro station (L2). It specialises in beef birria, alongside tacos al pastor, cochinita pibil, vegetarian tacos, margaritas and micheladas. Matches are shown on a big screen. Group menus for 6 to 50 guests start at €28 per person. Open Wednesday to Sunday; closed Mondays and Tuesdays.
