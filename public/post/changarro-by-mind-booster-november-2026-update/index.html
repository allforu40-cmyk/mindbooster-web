Changarro by Mind Booster: November 2026 update

Changarro: from pilot kitchen to its first founding restaurants

At Mind Booster we build Changarro, the management software for independent restaurants: take a photo of a delivery note and Changarro tells you your stock, what each dish really costs you and when a supplier raises a price. It also handles staff clock-ins and the team rota.

This month Changarro moved from building to selling. It runs every day in a Barcelona restaurant, which helps us test every feature in a real kitchen, and we have started presenting it to other restaurants in the city.

What's new in Changarro

  • Products and recipes by hand, without waiting for a delivery note: for market runs, one-off purchases or samples.
  • Shift rota by team: see only front of house or only the kitchen, and change several of one person's shifts at once.
  • Delivery notes that add up: if the photo misses the bottom of the page, Changarro calculates the subtotal, VAT and total from each line's prices; if the totals are there, it compares them and flags any difference.
  • Groups with several companies: when a delivery note doesn't say which company it's for, Changarro asks instead of guessing.

Now on Instagram and Facebook too

We've launched the Changarro presentation for restaurants and a one-minute video that explains it. If you see our ad, leave your details and we'll call you within 24 hours.

We're looking for 10 founding restaurants

The first 10 restaurants in Barcelona get 50% off for 12 months and a 30-day free trial, so they can see their real numbers before paying anything.

How you can help

  1. If you own or run an independent restaurant in Barcelona (1 to 3 locations), we'll show it to you in 15 minutes using your own delivery notes.
  2. If you know someone like that, introduce us: an email to hola@changarro.es with their name and a phone number is enough.
  3. If you're an accounting firm, adviser or hospitality supplier, let's talk: your clients can have their delivery notes and costs in order.
